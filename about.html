Workflow

Five Steps to
Publish-Ready Content

1

Select Your Sources

Choose from 15 curated crypto news outlets. Filter by recency — last 6h, 12h, 24h, or 48h. Add your own topic keywords to focus the analysis.

2

AI Analyzes the Feed

ChainReporter fetches live RSS data, pools the freshest articles, and sends them to GPT-4o-mini for virality scoring, impact assessment, and tweet generation.

3

Choose the Best Story

Ranked cards appear in the center panel. Tap any card to promote it to the Chosen One panel, review key takeaways, sentiment analysis, and the pre-written tweet.

4

Generate the Image

Select an image model (gpt-image-2, dall-e-3, and more) and generate a cinematic editorial image. Regenerate until it matches your vision.

5

Approve & Archive

Approve the image and tweet. ChainReporter saves the image to Google Drive and logs the story — headline, tweet, source, score, and image link — to your Google Sheet automatically.
